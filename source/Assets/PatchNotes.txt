# Patch Notes V1.3.1:

## New:

### Separate Audio Interface Channels
In Settings → Audio lanes, open Choose channels on a compatible Windows input to record selected channels as separate tracks. Each channel can have its own name, meter, and mute control.

## QOL Changes:

### Audio Device Changes While Paused
Pause replay to change audio devices or channels. Mute and unmute still work during replay.
