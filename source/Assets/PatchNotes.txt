# Patch Notes V1.3:

## New:

### Multi-Segment Video Editor
Split a clip on the timeline, remove or restore sections, and merge adjacent segments with matching edits. Crop segments independently, or resize the export using a platform preset or custom dimensions.

### Display Selection
FLICK captures the Windows primary display by default. Pause replay to select another monitor. The status HUD and screenshots follow the selected display.

## QOL Changes:

### Clip Viewer Controls
Browse previous and next clips, open fullscreen, seek five seconds in either direction, and adjust volume from the keyboard. Press M or click the speaker icon to mute.

### Organized Settings
Settings are grouped by purpose, with an About section for project links.

## BUGFIXES:

### Screenshot Preview
Screenshots open inside FLICK, with improved capture handling for selected displays.

### In-App Updates
Improved how FLICK downloads, verifies, and hands off installers for in-app updates.
